02 / Principles

Built with restraint

01

Local when possible

Files and sensitive input stay on your device whenever the tool can operate without a server.

02

No forced accounts

Open a tool and use it. No registration wall, newsletter prompt, or artificial friction.

03

Focused by design

Each utility is built around a clear job instead of becoming a crowded all-purpose application.
